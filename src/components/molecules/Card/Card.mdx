# Card

<Localized
  en={
    <>
      <p>
        Use <strong>Card</strong> to group related content on a raised surface — a
        summary, a setting, or an item in a grid — with its title and actions kept
        together.
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>
          <strong>Three optional parts, in order.</strong> <code>Card.Header</code> holds
          the title, <code>Card.Body</code> the content and <code>Card.Footer</code> the
          actions. Use only the parts you need; they stack in the order you write them.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>A plain container.</strong> Card and its parts carry no meaning of their
          own for assistive technology. When the content stands on its own, such as a post
          in a feed or a product in a list, wrap the card in an{' '}
          <code>&lt;article&gt;</code> or a list item and give it a real heading.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>It reskins on its own.</strong> Surface, border, corners and shadow come
          from tokens, so Card follows the light/dark theme and the neutral/pixel skin
          with no CSS changes.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>It adapts to its own width.</strong> The padding grows once the card is
          wide enough, whatever the screen size, and footer actions wrap onto a new line
          on a narrow card.
        </li>
      </ul>
    </>
  }
  fr={

      </p>
