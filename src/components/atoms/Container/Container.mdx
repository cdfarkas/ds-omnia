# Container

<Localized
  en={
    <>
      <p>
        Use <strong>Container</strong> to hold a page section at a comfortable reading
        width: it caps how wide the content gets, centres it, and keeps even gutters down
        the sides.
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>
          <strong>Five widths.</strong> <code>variantSize</code> goes <code>sm</code>,{' '}
          <code>md</code>, <code>lg</code>, <code>xl</code>; the default is{' '}
          <code>md</code>. <code>full</code> removes the cap and spans the whole area.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Gutters that breathe.</strong> The side padding is small on phones and
          widens on larger screens, so content never touches the edge.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Layout only.</strong> It has no colour and no top or bottom spacing —
          put those on what you place inside it.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>It is a plain box.</strong> Container renders a <code>div</code> and
          passes your props through, so add <code>role</code>, <code>aria-*</code> or an{' '}
          <code>id</code> when the section needs them.
        </li>
      </ul>
    </>
  }
  fr={
